# Simple Email Setup - Web3Forms

## 🚀 Quick Setup (5 minutes)

### Step 1: Get Access Key
1. Go to [Web3Forms.com](https://web3forms.com/)
2. Enter your email: **nileshgirissism@gmail.com**
3. Get your free access key

### Step 2: Update Configuration
Replace `YOUR_ACCESS_KEY` in `src/lib/emailService.ts` with your actual key:

```typescript
access_key: 'your-actual-access-key-here'
```

### Step 3: Test
- Fill out contact form
- Emails will be sent to **nileshgirissism@gmail.com**
- No additional setup required!

## ✅ Benefits
- ✅ No dependencies needed
- ✅ Free (1000 emails/month)
- ✅ No registration required
- ✅ Works immediately
- ✅ Spam protection included

## 🔧 Alternative: Formspree
